## 🎮 About the Game

No More Room in Hell 2 is a cooperative first-person zombie survival horror game developed by Torn Banner Studios. It entered Early Access on October 22, 2024 and fully launched on August 11, 2026. Players control Responders who complete objectives, gather supplies, manage infection, and extract while facing hordes of undead.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **No More Room in Hell 2**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for private, offline, or controlled testing sessions. It can reduce the pressure of stamina management, ammunition scarcity, infection progression, and character survival while preserving the game's core movement, inventory, combat, objective, and extraction systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to practice combat, test weapons and attachments, explore maps, learn objective routes, study infection and extraction systems, or create controlled private-session scenarios.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Movement combines walking, sprinting, jumping, vaulting, and stamina management. Sprinting and melee actions draw from the same stamina pool, so normal play requires careful timing. Enable Infinite Stamina when practicing routes, testing sprint distances, or learning how to reposition around runners and larger groups of zombies.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory space is limited, making supply management important during missions. Weapons, ammunition, medical items, infection treatments, explosives, and other equipment compete for available slots. Use the inventory-related options for controlled loadout testing, but remember that supplies normally contribute to Credits only when the team completes final extraction.

### World & Environment

Objective missions use large maps with a sequence of tasks that culminates in an extraction window. Survival missions focus on defending speaker systems against multiple zombie waves. Explore gradually, collect supplies, locate medical resources, and identify extraction points before escalating encounters. Fast Interaction can help test objectives without removing the need to understand mission routes.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat includes firearms, weapon attachments, melee weapons, explosives, and environmental positioning. Ammunition is limited, melee attacks consume stamina, and zombie attacks can cause infection. Use God Mode, Unlimited Ammunition, or No Reload for weapon testing and combat practice. Disable assistance features when you want to experience the intended resource pressure and permadeath risks.
